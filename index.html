DECODING BRIEF

Reveal the image from left to right

Use R or Python. Do not resize, crop, colour-correct or save with lossy compression between stages.

1

Left band · modular subtraction

For every red, green and blue value, subtract the position-dependent shift used during encoding. Wrap the result into 0–255.

Exact rule

Use zero-based coordinates x, y and channel c, where R=0, G=1, B=2. Let d0…d7 be the student-number digits.

k = d[(x + y + c) mod 8]
shift = (17k + 3x + 5y + 29c) mod 256
decoded = (encoded − shift) mod 256

2

Middle band · XOR and channel rotation

Remove the XOR mask first. Then rotate the three colour channels back to their original positions.

Exact rule

rotation = 1 + (d[(x + y) mod 8] mod 2)
mask(c) = (31d[(2x + y + c) mod 8] + 7x + 11y + 13c) mod 256

XOR each encoded channel with its mask. If rotation=1, rotate the result right by one channel; if rotation=2, rotate it right by two channels.

3

Right band · row swaps and XOR

Within each column, swap rows 0–4 with 5–9, then 10–14 with 15–19, and continue in blocks of ten. Leave a final incomplete block unchanged. Remove the XOR mask after swapping.

Exact rule

Swap complete five-pixel halves for all four channels, including alpha.

Then, for R, G and B only: decoded = swapped XOR mask(c), where mask(c) = (29d[(x + 2y + c) mod 8] + 13x + 17y + 19c) mod 256.

How to find the three bands

Let q = floor(width ÷ 3) and r = width mod 3. Band 1 has q + 1 columns when r ≥ 1; band 2 has q + 1 columns when r = 2; band 3 receives the rest. Start at column 0.
